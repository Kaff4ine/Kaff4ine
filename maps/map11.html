gm_bulkhead2048

Garry's Mod

Released: 2026-07-08




gm_bulkhead2048 was made for the 2048 cubed mapping competition on the hammer editor discord group server

The competition didn't have a specific theme, but the restriction is that your maps' playable area must fit in a 2048x2048x2048 cube (roughly 50x50x50 meters)


When it came to thinking of what to add on the map I thought to myself that most gmod maps have either a central area that leads outwards

to small ones, or a linear progression through it. So I went completely against the standard and made a darksouls 1-like map that tries

to have as many branching paths and interconnected areas/corridors
